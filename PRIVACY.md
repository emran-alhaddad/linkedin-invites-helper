# Privacy Policy

**LinkedIn Invites Helper** does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

- No analytics
- No remote servers of its own
- No user identifiers
- Runs only on two LinkedIn pages: My Network → Grow, and Invitation Manager → Received

How it works: when you click **Accept all** or **Ignore all**, the extension sends the same
accept/ignore requests that LinkedIn's own website sends, directly from your browser to
linkedin.com, using your existing LinkedIn session. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

To resume large jobs, it saves its progress (counts and invitation IDs, no names or messages)
in your browser's local storage for linkedin.com. Clicking **Discard** or finishing a job deletes it.

For questions: open an issue on the repository or email the maintainer.
